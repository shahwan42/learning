# Track A — Working with AI (the new engineering loop)

The spine of the project: how LLMs change the day-to-day practice of building products.

| # | Topic | Status | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The LLM mental model — tokens, context, sampling, why hallucination is structural | 🔄 in progress | One model that predicts every AI tool's behavior; the durable core under tool churn |
| A2 | Context engineering — the window as a budget; what your agent actually sees; CLAUDE.md; session degradation & compaction | ⬜ | The single biggest lever on agent output quality |
| A3 | Driving coding agents — task sizing, plan-then-execute, spec-first prompting, steering & interrupting | ⬜ | Turns "the AI wrote garbage" into repeatable delegation |
| A4 | Verification discipline — reviewing AI diffs, hallucinated APIs, plausible-but-wrong code, tests as the contract | ⬜ | Your job shifts from writing to verifying; this is the new bottleneck |
| A5 | Spec-driven & parallel workflows — specs as source of truth, subagents/worktrees, what to delegate vs keep | ⬜ | Scales you from one task at a time to a portfolio |
| A6 | Prototype→production — what AI speed changes (idea→demo) and what it doesn't (review, ops, migrations); hardening AI-written code | ⬜ | Where teams actually get burned |

Notes live in `notes/`, one file per topic, written when the topic is taught.
