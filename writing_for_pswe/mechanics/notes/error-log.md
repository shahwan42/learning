# Personal error log

Observability for your writing: every exercise review appends or increments here. Patterns that stop appearing get struck through. Frequency = priority.

| Pattern | Example (yours) | Fix | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing article before singular count noun | "we spend good portion of our day" | "a good portion" | 1 |
| much/many with count nouns | "so much ai-based workflows" | "so many AI-based workflows" | 1 |
| Collocation: "for now" vs "so far" | "we've built … for now" | "so far" = up to now; "for now" = temporarily | 1 |
| Meaning-flipping typo survives self-review | "now what we should tell it to do" ("not"?) | add a proofread pass that reads for meaning, not spelling | 1 |
| Casual register in a formal ask | "etc...", closing "Thanks" | cut "etc"; close with the ask or next step | 1 |
| Inconsistent capitalization of terms | "Claude-API", "claude", "ai-based" | pick one form per term, apply everywhere | 1 |
