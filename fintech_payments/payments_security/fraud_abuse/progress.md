# Track 4 — Fraud & Abuse (defender side)

What attackers do with a working checkout and real accounts, and the engineering controls that make it unprofitable.

## Topics
| # | Topic | Status | Competency check |
|---|-------|--------|------------------|
| 1 | Card testing | not started | Attack anatomy (why your checkout is the oracle); detection signals (auth-rate, BIN velocity); where rate limits and CAPTCHA go without killing conversion |
| 2 | Account takeover | not started | Credential stuffing vs phishing paths; session hardening; step-up auth triggers before money moves |
| 3 | Risk engines | not started | Rules vs scores; how PSP tools (Radar-style) slot into your flow; tuning false positives; review queues |
| 4 | Disputes & chargebacks | not started | Reason-code taxonomy, evidence that wins, friendly fraud; how CVV/AVS/3DS choices upstream change dispute outcomes |
| 5 | Fraud observability | not started | Metrics that matter (auth rate, dispute rate, decline mix), alert design, feedback loop from disputes back into rules |

Synthesis exercise: fraud defense plan for launching a new online checkout in a new market.
