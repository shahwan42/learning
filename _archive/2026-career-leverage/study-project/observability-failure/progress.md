# Track D — Observability & Failure

**Framing:** observability is not "add logging." It's the ability to answer questions about your system that you didn't think of in advance. This track is the debugging half of seniority.

**Done when:** given a vague "the app is slow" page at 3am, you have a systematic loop that narrows to a cause in minutes, and you can defend your instrumentation's cardinality bill.
